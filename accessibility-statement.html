01

Our commitment to digital inclusion

We are dedicated to providing digital learning experiences that are accessible to everyone, regardless of ability or technology. We continuously work to improve the accessibility, clarity, and usability of our course portals, learner dashboards, and educational resources.

02

Accessibility standards & WCAG 2.1 AA conformance

We align our digital learning platforms and course interfaces with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA specifications. These international guidelines outline best practices for creating digital educational content that is Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, and Robust.

03

Assistive features & platform accommodations

  • High-contrast visual design, balanced typography, and responsive zoom support up to 200% without loss of content.
  • Full keyboard navigation across navigation menus, certificate finder tools, and course workspaces.
  • Alternative text provided for essential instructional diagrams, graphics, and course assets.
  • Standardized digital study guides and PDF materials structured for compatibility with modern screen-reading technologies.
  • Clear form labels, focused indicator rings, and predictable tab ordering throughout user workflows.
04

Technical specifications & compatible technologies

Platform accessibility relies on modern web standards including HTML5, semantic CSS3, WAI-ARIA attributes, and JavaScript. We recommend using current versions of major browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) alongside assistive tools (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver) for the best learning experience.

05

Feedback, requests & accessibility support

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or require course materials in an alternative format, please contact our support team at admissions@cambridgelearningservices.org or call 022-4150-4304 / 022-4150-4305.
