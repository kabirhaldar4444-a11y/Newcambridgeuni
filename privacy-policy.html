Who handles your data

02

Information you give us

  • Name, contact information, address and account credentials.
  • Billing profile, company details and tax identifiers.
  • A photo identity document used for KYC.
  • A brief video identity check and verification result.
  • Messages, complaints and supporting material sent to support.
03

Information the platform records

  • Orders, invoices, refunds and payment references.
  • Course access, progress, answers, marks and certificate records.
  • Device, browser, IP address, login, download and streaming events.
  • Error, fraud and security signals.
04

How it helps us serve you

We use information to register you, confirm identity, deliver protected material, provide examinations, create accurate invoices and certificates, answer support requests, prevent fraud and maintain reliable records. Processing is based on the learner contract, consent where necessary, legal obligations and legitimate security or operational interests as permitted locally.

05

Extra care for identity checks

ID documents and video verification deserve stronger protection. Access should be restricted to authorised staff and approved processors. They are used to match the learner to the account, reduce impersonation and preserve exam integrity.

Identity Verification & Data Protection

Identity checks are performed via secure encrypted digital verification channels. Verification data and video records are stored in secure cloud infrastructure and are de-identified or permanently purged within 90 days following course completion and certificate issuance, unless required longer for statutory compliance.

06

Who we work with

Selected payment, cloud-hosting, identity, email, security, analytics and professional advisers may process limited data for us under contract. Banks may receive relevant order and delivery evidence when investigating a dispute. Authorities receive information only where a lawful request applies.

We do not sell personal data or provide KYC data to unrelated advertising networks.

07

How long we keep it

Each category is kept only as long as needed for the service, legal accounts, certificate verification, fraud investigations or claims. Raw identity and video records should be removed or irreversibly de-identified once their documented purpose and mandatory period end.

08

Your controls

Depending on applicable law, you may request a copy, correction, deletion, restriction or transfer of your data; object to some uses; withdraw consent; or complain to a regulator. We verify identity before acting and may retain records that law or legitimate fraud-prevention needs require.

Use a unique password and tell us promptly if you believe the account has been compromised.

Platform Governance

Official policy framework of Cambridge Learning Services. For compliance questions or formal communications, contact our admissions and legal governance desk at admissions@cambridgelearningservices.org.
