Cancellations & Refund Reviews

A plain-language guide to final digital sales, support fixes and refund eligibility.

01 Terms & Conditions02 Privacy Policy03 Service Delivery04 Refund Policy05 Accessibility
The short version

Most orders are final after protected content opens. We will still fix delivery problems, investigate duplicate charges and honour rights required by law.

01

Why digital orders become final

Course files and streams can be copied once opened. When you request immediate supply and we release protected material, a change-of-mind cancellation is normally unavailable, subject always to mandatory consumer rights.

02

Reasons that usually do not qualify

  • You decide the self-paced format is not for you after access begins.
  • You expected a tutor or live classroom that was not listed.
  • You do not finish KYC, study or the examination on time.
  • You are unhappy with a score or hoped-for employment result.
  • Your own device, connection, software or storage prevents use.
  • You lose a file after a successful permitted download.
  • Your account is closed for serious misuse or fraud.
03

When we open a review

Ask us to review a duplicate charge, payment with no account creation, substantial non-delivery, a serious difference from the checkout description, a persistent platform fault or a remedy required by applicable law.

We may correct billing, restore or extend access, repeat the affected step, offer a suitable replacement or credit, or approve a partial or full refund.

04

Requesting immediate access

Where local law requires it, checkout will separately record that you want digital performance to start immediately and understand its effect on a cancellation period. That consent is linked to the order and timestamped.

05

How to ask

Contact admissions support at admissions@cambridgelearningservices.org within 7 calendar days of enrollment (prior to accessing or downloading protected course materials). Provide the order number, learner email, payment date and clear evidence of the problem. Do not send full card details, passwords or raw KYC files.

06

What happens next

We confirm the requester, examine payment and delivery records, try a technical correction where suitable and send our decision within 3 to 5 business days. Approved funds go back to the original method; your bank or gateway controls final posting time.

07

Payment disputes

You may use any genuine legal chargeback right. If a bank asks us to respond, we may supply relevant order acceptance, content-release timestamps and verification status. Deliberately false claims may be treated as account fraud.
