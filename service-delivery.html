The short version

Your admission starts after payment. The remaining steps verify your identity, release course assets, provide the exam and document your result.

01

The service format

This is an online, self-paced service. You work through recorded lessons and downloadable study guides in your own time, then take an online assessment. Any live element must be expressly listed in your order to be included.

02

Your delivery path

  1. WelcomePayment clears and the platform creates your admission confirmation and profile route.
  2. ID uploadYou submit an accepted photo ID through the secure verification area.
  3. Video checkYou record a short identity clip to confirm the registered learner.
  4. InvoiceYour applicable GST or tax invoice is normally posted within 24 hours after validation.
  5. Study filesYour PDF guides and workbooks are released. Save permitted downloads promptly.
  6. Enrolment proofThe dashboard generates a record confirming active course participation.
  7. Video lessonsPersonal access to the recorded learning pathway is enabled.
  8. Final testAfter the selected 10-, 20- or 30-day period, examination credentials appear.
  9. OutcomeYour submission is graded and an eligible result produces a provisional or completion certificate.
03

What keeps delivery moving

Complete KYC within the course period, use compatible software and a reliable connection, keep credentials private and complete the exam yourself. Report a missing item as soon as you notice it.

Some links are intentionally short-lived or single-use. That design protects licensed material. If a link fails because of our system, we will check the logs and provide an appropriate fix.

04

If something interrupts access

Maintenance, risk reviews or a security incident may temporarily pause delivery. For a material interruption caused by us, we can re-enable a stage, extend the window or provide another proportionate remedy.

Contact our support desk at admissions@cambridgelearningservices.org or call 022-4150-4304 with your order number. For your safety, upload identity files only through the approved secure route.

05

When fulfilment is complete

The purchased service is fulfilled when the learning files and video access have been made available for the agreed period and you have been given the promised assessment opportunity. Passing and certificate issuance remain subject to identity, submission and score requirements.
